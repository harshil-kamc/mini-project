    How to use

    • Move: drag an item. Resize: drag its white corner/edge squares.
    • Pan: drag empty ground (or right-drag). Zoom: mouse wheel.
    • ←↑→↓ nudge the selected item (Shift = 10× faster).
    • Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc deselect.
    • R or ⟳ Rotate 90° turns the selected item: buildings, roads, plazas, the ball court, parking, playground, garden, benches and NPC spots. The court lines, parking bays and cricket pitch turn with it.
    • Street lights, trees, benches, trash cans and rocks can be dragged, added, duplicated and deleted. Rocks have working solid collision; select one to toggle it.
    • Place an image from your computer, then drag or resize it. You can turn collision on for placed images, and delete or duplicate them like other items. Select the main gate to resize its image.
    • The green dividers in the middle of the roads are solid: no one can walk or drive over them. Select one to adjust its corner radius.
    • Both parking lots have car bays; the larger lot behind the library also has reserved bus bays. Move or resize a Parking area to change its slots.
    • The brown mark next to a building is its entrance. To change which way it faces, select the building and use the 🧭 buttons, or drag the round 🚪 handle to another side. Keep the space in front of it free.
    • Drag the main gate anywhere on the map. Use the Map X and Map Y fields for exact placement; compass buttons change which way it faces. The boundary wall stays intact when the gate is inside the map.
    • Hidden layers can't be clicked. Hide roads if you need to grab what's under them.
